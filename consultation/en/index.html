How it works

We choose a realistic scope for the three hours. The content follows your needs, rather than a fixed program for everyone.

  1. You prepare the groundwork.

  2. We choose where to start.

  3. We adapt the tools to your context.

    We review what we worked on and the steps you can continue after the consultation.
